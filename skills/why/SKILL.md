# Why

Answer from the record, not the code: read the commits and PR discussions that introduced the target and each later change. If that trail is thin, search the other sources this session can reach, such as the issue tracker, design docs, team chat, and error tracking. Cite what the authors wrote, mark your own inferences as inferences, and say what the record does not answer.
